Human control

Press Human (or H): WASD move · Shift run · E pick up / drop · V first-person · C shirt colour · drag mouse to look. The robot sees you with its ToF sensors and waits. Drive (or M) lets you steer the robot with the arrow keys (↑ forward, ↓ reverse, ← → turn, Shift boost) while you keep WASD for the human.
